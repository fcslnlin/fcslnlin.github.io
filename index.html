About

I'm a content strategist and producer based in Los Angeles with 8+ years of visual production experience across premium automotive, corporate, healthcare, and institutional brands.

For two years I was the sole in-house content creator at OpenRoad Auto Group in Vancouver — one of Canada's largest premium dealer groups — running content strategy and production across eight brands simultaneously. I didn't just shoot. I ran editorial calendars, aligned with stakeholders, optimized for Google PMAX, and reported monthly to general managers across the territory.

What I bring that most content marketers don't: I can handle the full pipeline myself. Brief, shoot, edit, publish.
