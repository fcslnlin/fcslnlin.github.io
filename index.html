About

Strategy first.

I'm a content strategist and producer based in Los Angeles with a background spanning premium automotive, corporate, healthcare, and institutional brands. For two years I was the in-house content creator at OpenRoad Auto Group in Vancouver — one of Canada's largest premium dealer groups — where I ran content strategy and production across eight brands at once.

Outside of OpenRoad I've worked on corporate productions with Defiant One Media, covering everything from international healthcare conferences to business school recap series — with UBC Sauder bringing me back four years running.
